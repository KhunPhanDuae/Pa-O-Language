Learn the basics of the Pa-O alphabet and pronunciation.

  • က - ka
  • ခ - kha

  • င - nga

Greetings and Common Expressions

Common greetings in Pa-O:

  • ခွဲးဟောဝ်ဒျာႏ (kawe-har-dyar) - Hello
  • အုံဟောဝ်နေꩻ (on hwan-nay) - How are you?
  • ကေႏဇူꩻတင်ႏငါႏ (kay-ju-tann-ngar) - Thank you
  • မွေး (mway) - Yes
  • မွေးတဝ်း (mway-tar) - No
  • တဆင်ႏ (ta-shan) - Please
  • တဆင်ႏမွိုင်ꩻ (ta shan mong) - Excuse me / Sorry

Self-Introduction

Introduce yourself in Pa'O Karen:

  • ခွေမဉ်ꩻနဝ်ꩻ...ဒျာႏသွူ - My name is...
  • နာꩻမဉ်ꩻနုဲင်းမုဲင်ꩻ - What is your name?

Pronouns

  • I / Me: ခွေ (kway)
  • You: နာꩻ (na)
  • He / She / It: ဝွေꩻ (way)
  • We / Us: နီ (ni)
  • They / Them: ဝွေꩻသီး (way zee)

Basic Verbs & Simple Sentences

Basic Verbs

  • Eat: အံႏ (aan)
  • Drink: အွꩻ (aw)
  • Sleep: ဗေင်ႏ (bain)
  • Go: လွေꩻ (lway)
  • Come: လွဉ် (lawn)

Statements

  • I want to eat.: ခွေမဲဉ်အံႏငါႏ (kway men aan ngar)
  • You are going.: နာꩻအောဝ်ႏလွေꩻထာꩻ (na eor lway htar)
  • He is sleeping.: ဝွေꩻအောဝ်ႏဗေင်ႏထာꩻ (way eor-bain-htar)

Questions

  • Are you hungry?: နာꩻဟို့ꩻခိုးနေꩻဒဲဉ်? (na hoet koe nay den)
  • Where are you going?: နာꩻအီႏလွေꩻမုဲင်ꩻ? (na aee-lway-mai)
  • What is your name?: နာꩻမဉ်ꩻတမုဲင်ꩻ (na-me ta-mai)

Numbers

  • One: တာ (ta)
  • Two: နီ (ni)
  • Three: သွံ (shone)
  • Four: လစ်ꩻ (lea)
  • Five: ငတ်ꩻ (ngat)
